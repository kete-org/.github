# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Kete is building software for businesses and developers everywhere, and we want our community to be just as welcoming. Everyone who takes part in our repositories, issues, discussions and other spaces should be able to do so without harassment or discrimination. That applies regardless of background, identity, experience level, nationality, age, disability, appearance, or personal beliefs.

## What we expect

- **Be respectful.** Disagree about ideas, not people. Assume good intent.
- **Be constructive.** Give feedback that helps someone improve their work, and accept feedback on yours graciously.
- **Be inclusive.** Help newcomers find their way. There are no stupid questions.
- **Be considerate.** Remember that contributors work across time zones, languages and cultures.
- **Own your mistakes.** Apologise when you get something wrong and learn from it.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances of any kind
- Threats, intimidation, or encouraging others to do these things
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated off-topic posting
- Any other behaviour that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Where this applies

This Code of Conduct applies in all kete-org spaces: repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions and comments. It also applies when someone is representing Kete publicly, for example at events or on official social media accounts.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please email **conduct@your-website.com**. Include links, screenshots or other context if you can.

All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as possible. We will not tolerate retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate in response to a violation, including:

1. **A private warning** explaining the problem and the behaviour expected in future.
2. **A temporary restriction** from participating in kete-org spaces.
3. **A permanent ban** for serious or repeated violations.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face the same consequences.

## Questions

If something here is unclear, or you think it could be improved, contact **conduct@your-website.com**.
